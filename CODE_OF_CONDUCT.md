# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated off-topic noise
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

**To report a problem,** use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/xkid1/reed-solomon-alg-mcp/security/advisories/new) and mark the report as a conduct issue, or contact the maintainer [@xkid1](https://github.com/xkid1) through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

Maintainers will respond with proportionate action: a private note, a warning, a temporary block, or a permanent ban.
